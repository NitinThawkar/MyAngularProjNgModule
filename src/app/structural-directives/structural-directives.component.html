✨structural-directives ✨

Structural Directives denoted by * are used to conditionally include or exclude elements from the DOM. *ngIf, *ngFor, *ngSwitch, *ngTemplate, *ngContainer, *ngTemplateOutlet, *ngPlural are some commonly used structural directives in Angular.
Structural Directives - modify the DOM
Attribute Directives - modify the properties of the DOM elements
